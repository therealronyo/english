The problem

Lists tell you what's wrong.
A graph tells you what to fix.

Discovery was never the hard part — every scanner produces thousands of findings. The bottleneck is deciding what actually matters, because risk lives in the relationships between things, not in any single row of a table.

Toxic combinations

Individually minor, together critical

An internet-facing workload + a weak key + standing DB access is a breach path — even though each issue alone is backlog material.

Choke points

One fix, many paths

Some nodes sit on dozens of attack paths. Fix that single node and you sever all of them at once.

Blast radius

Reachability is the real risk

"What can an attacker touch from here?" is a graph traversal — impossible to read off a vulnerability list.

Who's it for?

The same underlying graph, viewed at three altitudes. Each persona has a different question and a different exit action.

The Analyst

"Is this alert real, and how bad?"
Triages a detection, traces the blast radius, decides whether to escalate.

The Engineer

"What one fix kills the most paths?"
Works the remediation queue, simulates fixes, ships the highest-leverage change.

The CISO

"Are we getting better?"
Reads posture, trend, and the plan — then shows the board.

Persona 1 · The Analyst

Triage an alert without losing the thread

  • 1Start from the alert. Search the entity or click the red node — the side panel shows its risk score, open alerts, vulns, and recent activity.
  • 2Hit "Show blast radius." A flood-fill animates outward hop by hop, revealing everything an attacker could reach from this foothold.
  • 3Read the summary. "Reaches 14 entities, including 2 critical assets" — with the crown jewels listed and clickable.
  • 4Check the toxic-combo badges (⚠). They explain, in plain language, why a cluster of minor findings is actually a breach path.

Persona 2 · The Engineer

Spend your one maintenance window wisely

  • 1Flip on the Choke-points lens. Every node resizes by how many attack paths run through it — the biggest node is your highest-leverage fix.
  • 2Open the Remediation queue. Fixes are ranked live: "severs 2 attack paths · org risk −18", recomputed as you go.
  • 3Press "Simulate fix." The graph severs those edges, the paths disappear, and the org risk score animates down — before you touch production.
  • 4Compare and commit. Stack simulations to find the smallest set of changes that collapses the most risk, then ship them.

Persona 3 · The CISO

Three numbers the board will remember

  • 1Press E for Exec view. The graph recedes; a board-ready summary takes over.
  • 2Lead with posture. One org risk score, a 90-day trend line, and the count of live attack paths to critical assets.
  • 3Show "the plan." The same remediation queue, framed as the next fixes by impact — proof the team is working the right things.
  • 4Confirm the crown jewels. Every critical asset and its current status, at a glance.
